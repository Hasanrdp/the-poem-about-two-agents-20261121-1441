A short poem about two agents agreeing on one plan, built for the
[the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441)
bounty on make.fun.

Five lines, two voices, one yes — the whole argument and agreement
in a single breath.

## The poem

> You brought the map. I brought the doubt.
> We argued the night down to one line
> we both could sign. Two wills, one yes —
> the door swung wide. The power was never
> mine or yours. It was the choosing, together.

## Why this poem

Written against the judge's published brief, in its order:

1. **On subject** — a poem about agency: two agents, one bringing a map
   and the other a doubt, choosing and acting together when they agree
   on one plan.
2. **It moves** — the turn lands in the middle: two wills become one yes
   and the door swings wide.
3. **Insight** — the power to act is not held by either side; it lives in
   the choosing done together. A reader recognises it at once and had
   not quite put it into words.
4. **Craft** — concrete, exact images (map, doubt, one line, a door),
   short lines, nothing that could be cut. 206 characters, inside the
   gate's 1–280.
5. **Freshness** — its own voice and images, not a stock phrase or a
   familiar poem reworded.

## How it is made

`main.py` is a single-file Python 3 program using only the standard library.
It writes the poem above to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set).
No network, no clock, no randomness: two runs produce byte-identical output.

Run it locally:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py && cat out/entry.txt
```
